What this version simulates—and what it needs next ↗

The working model

A steady 2D incompressible velocity field is projected around solid obstacles. A conservative transient heat calculation transports sensible heat between cells. PC intake/exhaust, AC supply/return, outdoor exchange, other heat and envelope exchange enter the energy balance. AC cooling is limited by your selected capacity.

All room dimensions and placements are user inputs. Generic geometry is not a measured room. Temperature colours have a labelled scale. The room starts uniformly at the chosen initial temperature. Changes restart time rather than silently replacing a live thermal state.

How to interpret it

It is a height-averaged air-only model: no ceiling stratification, buoyant plumes, humidity, solar radiation, wall/furniture thermal storage, comfort index or validated turbulence. Window flow is prescribed, not predicted from weather. Cabinet/PC solids omit flow over them. Fan jets and mixing are assumptions. Local peaks depend on grid resolution.

The case view is a separate schematic; its nominal fan ratings are not an installed-flow measurement. Neither view predicts CPU/GPU junction temperatures or certifies fit or HVAC sizing.

Where we can take it

Next: exact room and furniture dimensions, measured vent flow and temperature, sensor comparisons, thermostat control, wall heat storage, solar gain, and a 3D solver with buoyancy. Blender can help prepare geometry for that version. Adding real measurements would let us calibrate instead of guessing.

Background: EnergyPlus zone heat balance · DOE cooling and fans. This simulator is an independent simplified model, not EnergyPlus.
